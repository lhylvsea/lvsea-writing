# English AI-smell notes

English drafts use the same fact and intent rules as Chinese drafts, but read this file when the target text is English.

- Remove chat residue, fake citations, empty authority, generic openers, consultant-speak, vague abstractions, and over-balanced caveats.
- Prefer concrete subjects and verbs over leverage, empower, unlock, facilitate, robust, seamless, game-changing, landscape, delve, foster, crucial, and similar stock language when the word adds no precision.
- Break false ranges, overly symmetrical "not X, but Y" constructions, repeated headings, and lists that should be sentences.
- Preserve product names, technical terms, links, citations, code, and house style when they are real requirements.
- Do not make English deliberately broken. Human texture is opt-in and must not change numbers, names, code, links, claims, or meaning.
